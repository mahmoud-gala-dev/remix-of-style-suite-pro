## Improvements Implemented

### `/bi` — Business Intelligence
- **KPIs**: active customers, avg LTV, booking→completed conversion %,
  no-show rate.
- **Conversion funnel** (Booked → Confirmed → Arrived → Completed) as
  horizontal Recharts bar.
- **RFM segmentation** (Recency / Frequency / Monetary) bucketing
  customers into 7 segments: champions, loyal, potential, new,
  needsAttention, atRisk, hibernating. Pie chart + per-segment counts.
- **Monthly retention cohorts** (last 6 cohorts × M0–M3) with
  heatmap-shaded percentages.
- **Top 10 customers by LTV** table with R/F/M columns.

### `/accounting` — P&L, Cash Flow, QuickBooks Export
- Date-range filter (default last 30 days).
- **KPIs**: revenue, costs (heuristic), net income, net margin.
- **Daily cash flow** line chart.
- **P&L statement** with revenue, revenue-by-category breakdown,
  commissions (35% est.), overhead (15% est.), net income.
- **Revenue-by-category** table with % share.
- **Export P&L (CSV)** — section/account/amount.
- **Export Journal (CSV)** — QuickBooks/Zoho-compatible double-entry
  journal (Date, JournalNo, Account, Description, Debit, Credit,
  Currency) — one debit Cash + credit Revenue:`<category>` per
  completed booking.
